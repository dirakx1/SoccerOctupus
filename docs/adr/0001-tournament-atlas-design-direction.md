# Use Tournament Atlas as the frontend design direction

The client selected Tournament Atlas after comparing Signal Desk, Match Centre,
Analyst Ledger, Swarm Orbit, Stadium Ribbon, and Tournament Atlas mockups. The
production redesign will use Tournament Atlas because its competition-first
hierarchy supports both tournament and league editions while keeping standings,
predictions, simulations, and markets part of one recognisable product system.

## Consequences

- Competition context is a first-class part of the application shell.
- Editorial scale and bracket-inspired pathways are used selectively; dense
  operational views still prioritise scanning and accessibility.
- Light and dark themes share the same semantic tokens and hierarchy.
- The mockup is a direction, not production code. Production modules must use
  real data, complete states, localization, and shared design-system interfaces.
